Frequently Asked Questions

What are your meditation mats made of? +
Our Premium Sage Mats are made from 100% natural tree rubber and a vegan polyurethane top layer. They are PVC-free and biodegradable.
Do you ship internationally? +
Yes, we currently ship to North America, Europe, Australia, and select parts of Asia. Shipping rates are calculated at checkout.
How do I clean my journal? +
The linen cover is treated to be water-resistant. Simply wipe with a damp, lint-free cloth. Do not use harsh chemicals.

Shipping & Returns

Processing Times

All orders are processed within 1-2 business days. Orders are not shipped or delivered on weekends or holidays.

Our Return Policy

We have a 30-day return policy, which means you have 30 days after receiving your item to request a return. To be eligible for a return, your item must be in the same condition that you received it, unworn or unused, with tags, and in its original packaging.
